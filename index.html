001/ The problem

Electronics businesses keep running into the same wall: expensive components, supply shortages and slow searches for compatible alternatives. Each one delays development and adds cost. Our proposed engine helps you discover and compare alternatives based on technical specifications, performance, reliability and affordability.

Status: proposed solution. In development.
